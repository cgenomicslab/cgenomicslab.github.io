<h1 class="section-title">Join the Lab</h1>

<div class="join-content">
<p>
We are committed to maintaining a supportive and collaborative lab environment for all members.
We are always interested in motivated and talented people, even if no position is currently advertised. Get in touch to discuss your scientific background and your goals for the future (in academia, industry or other ventures): send your CV to <a href="/contact/">Alexandros Pittis</a>.
</p>

<h2 class="subsection-title">Positions</h2>

<ul>
<li><strong>Undergraduate students</strong>: internships</li>
<li><strong>MSc students</strong>: thesis projects or internships</li>
<li><strong>PhD students</strong></li>
<li><strong>Postdoctoral researchers</strong></li>
<li><strong>Research technicians</strong> and visiting scholars</li>
</ul>

<p>MSc students can join through the University of Crete graduate programmes we teach in (see <a href="/teaching/">Teaching</a>).</p>
